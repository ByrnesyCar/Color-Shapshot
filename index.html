Your color clues · 3 of 4

Which feels more like you?

There are no right answers. Choose what tends to make you look most naturally alive and balanced—not simply the color you like best.

1. Which metal tends to flatter you most?
Think about what looks harmonious next to your skin.
2. Which color family makes you look healthier and fresher?
Imagine these colors near your face in natural light.
3. Do softer or clearer colors feel more natural on you?
This is about how much intensity your coloring seems to carry. We calibrated this clue gently so it informs rather than dominates your result.
4. How much natural contrast do you see in your features?
Think about the difference between your skin and your natural eyes, brows and hair.
