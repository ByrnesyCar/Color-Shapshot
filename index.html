Hosted Photo Analysis Prototype

This prototype detects facial landmarks in the browser, samples several landmark-relative facial regions, and computes raw lightness, chroma and contrast observations locally. Temperature is deliberately marked low-confidence and is not treated as a skin-tone classification.
